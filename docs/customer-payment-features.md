# Customer payment features — 20 September 2026

Groups accept an optional image banner. The course catalogue uses the earliest upcoming active group's banner. `/media/group_banners/<path>` serves only images referenced by a Group, including when Django DEBUG is false. Other media is unaffected.

The Mini App shows confirmed payment amount, cumulative paid amount and remaining purchase balance. A stored checkout attempt restores this result when the customer returns from the external payment page. Switching from family to self ignores abandoned family inputs.

Each participant can select one social category: pensioner, disability, or student. A PDF/JPEG/PNG document up to 5 MB is required before the additional 100,000 UZS discount is applied. It is capped at the participant's remaining unit price and stacks with the applicable family discount. The server validates document ownership and participant phone; discounts and proof references are snapshotted on the purchase. Booking discount cannot exceed the resulting total. Documents use PRIVATE_DOCUMENT_ROOT (default `BASE_DIR/private_documents`), outside public media; authorized staff can download them from the associated client card after payment. Sellers can access documents for their own clients only.

Every operator has a unique persistent referral code. Set `TELEGRAM_BOT_USERNAME` to the actual username. `/referrals/` displays `https://t.me/<bot>?start=ref_<code>` plus completed registrations, paid purchases and collected amount. Only the first bot registration accepts attribution; existing accounts cannot be reassigned by opening another link. New purchases snapshot the referrer. Confirmed, non-demo, non-refunded invoices count toward the seller's existing salary percentage calculation. No separate fixed referral bonus is introduced.

`/payments/` combines initial CRM payments, later subpayments, and Rahmat invoices. Initial payments exclude approved subpayment amounts to prevent double counting. Filters include method (cash, terminal, Rahmat), status, date and search. New manual payment forms offer these three methods. Historical methods are retained; old initial payments whose method was never recorded are labeled accordingly rather than guessed. Approval and refund actions remain on the linked detail pages; separate queue navigation is removed.

Confirmed non-demo invoices create one durable PaymentQRDelivery per member. The existing Telegram campaign worker processes these deliveries, with a lease and exponential retry delay. Each QR contains the CRM client's UUID, and the caption states the purchase's paid amount and remaining debt. Repeated callbacks do not create duplicate deliveries. Previously settled invoices are not backfilled. QR verification displays Mini App purchases as well as CRM payments. As with any external delivery, a crash after Telegram accepts a message but before the send is recorded can cause a retry.
